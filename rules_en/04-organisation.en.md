## Participation  
<span id="ORG-1"></span>  

### Conditions  
<span id="ORG-1.1"></span>  

**ORG-1.1.1**  
The competition is open to persons who are 18 years old (in special cases 16 years old).  

**ORG-1.1.1**  
Participation while under the influence of alcohol or performance-enhancing drugs is forbidden (including recreational substances and doctor-prescribed medications).

## Organisation and classification of competitions  
<span id="ORG-2"></span>  

### Individual pool bouts  
<span id="ORG-2.1"></span>  

**ORG-2.1.1**  
In each event that includes pools, pools consist of 7 fencers if the number of participants is divisible by 7; otherwise, pools of 7 and 6 fencers must be formed.  

**ORG-2.1.2**  
Before the start of the events, the Organising Team decides and announces how many fencers are eliminated based on the established ranking (0–40%).  

**ORG-2.1.3**  
After the pools, an overall ranking is established from the bout sheets using the following indicators in order: V/M, HS – HR, HS. (V = victories; M = matches; HS = hits scored; HR = hits received.)  

**ORG-2.1.4**  
The overall ranking table is formed as follows:  
- Compute the two indices (V/M, HS–HR) by aggregating the pool summary values.  
- The primary index is computed as victories divided by matches, per the V/M formula.  
- The fencer with the highest index (maximum 1) takes first place.  
- If multiple fencers have the same primary index, the secondary index HS–HR is used to rank them, i.e. hits scored minus hits received.  
- If both indices are equal, the fencer who scored more hits is ranked higher.  
- In case of absolute equality between two or more fencers, the order is determined by drawing lots.  

**ORG-2.1.5**  
If there is absolute equality among the last qualified fencers, all fencers with the same score qualify even if this exceeds the pre-announced number of qualifiers.  

**ORG-2.1.6**  
Non-attendance: a fencer who does not appear at the start of the pools is removed from the pool and recorded at the end of the final ranking without placement as “Did not start” / “Nem indult”.  

**ORG-2.1.7**  
If a fencer withdraws from exactly one pool bout due to equipment failure or injury but can complete their remaining bouts, the opponent is recorded as the winner (Vn–Dn / VN–Dn); however, the points scored are not deleted.  

#### Withdrawal  

**ORG-2.1.8**  
If a fencer withdraws from exactly one pool bout due to equipment failure or injury but can complete their remaining bouts, the opponent is recorded as the winner (Vn–Dn / VN–Dn); however, the points scored are not deleted.  

**ORG-2.1.9**  
If a fencer withdraws from the event during the pools, all their results up to that point are annulled and all opponents are recorded as winners V0–D0 in every pool bout, disregarding the prior results. The withdrawing fencer is not included in establishing the direct elimination ranking, and is recorded in the final ranking as “Did not finish” / “Visszalépett”.  

**ORG-2.1.10**  
If a fencer would withdraw only from their last pool bout, results are handled as if they withdrew from a single bout (see above), but they do not participate in direct elimination; they are recorded as “Did not finish” / “Visszalépett”.  

**ORG-2.1.11**  
Exclusion: a fencer who is excluded during the pools is removed from the pool; their results are annulled as if they had not participated. In the final ranking they are recorded without placement as “Excluded”.  

### Individual direct elimination bouts  
<span id="ORG-2.2"></span>  

**ORG-2.2.1**  
The complete or incomplete direct elimination table must be created according to the ranking and the rules of the event (see e.g. Appendix/IndDE64.pdf).  

**ORG-2.2.2**  
Organisers publish the schedule/plan for the direct elimination bouts.  

**ORG-2.2.3**  
If a fencer withdraws during direct elimination, the opponent is declared the winner, but the withdrawing fencer does not lose their ranking position.  

**ORG-2.2.4**  
Withdrawal: if for any reason a fencer cannot continue or finish the bout, the opponent is declared the winner. The withdrawing fencer keeps their placement in the overall ranking and is recorded as “Did not finish” / “Visszalépett”.  

**ORG-2.2.5**  
Exclusion: if a fencer is excluded, they must be removed from the direct elimination table and their results annulled as if they had not participated. In the final ranking they are recorded at the end as “Excluded” / “Kizárva”.  

**ORG-2.2.6**  
Order of bouts: in each round of the direct elimination table (256, 128, 64, 32, 16, 8, 4) bouts are announced strictly from top to bottom according to the bracket.  

#### The final  

**ORG-2.2.7**  
The final is run either as direct elimination or as fencing for places, and should preferably consist of 4 fencers.  

**ORG-2.2.8**  
Finals for women’s and men’s events of the same weapon should preferably be held on the same day.  

### Team competitions  
<span id="ORG-2.3"></span>  

**ORG-2.3.1**  
Teams consist of three fencers for each weapon category, with or without one reserve. A team may only start a match if all members are present.  

**ORG-2.3.2**  
Team competition is fenced as a direct elimination bracket which may include byes.  

**ORG-2.3.3**  
Team seeding on the bracket is determined as follows:  

- Use the average of the team members’ current `weightedRating` on HEMARatings (sum of `relativeRating` divided by team size).  
- If a fencer has results in both ‘Mixed/Men’ and ‘Women’, the higher `weightedRating` is used.  
- Team members without a HEMARating are assigned a value equal to half of the worst `weightedRating` in the relevant category (for mixed teams: ‘Mixed/Men’, regardless of gender).  
- If two teams’ computed indices are equal, their order is determined by drawing lots.

**ORG-2.3.4**  
For the first 16 places in the bracket, teams fence for all places. From 17th place downward, team placement is determined by their original bracket position.  

#### The competition is conducted as follows  

**ORG-2.3.5**  
The relay system applies to all weapon categories.  

**ORG-2.3.6**  
The three fencers of one team fence the three fencers of the other team (9 relay bouts).  

**ORG-2.3.7**  
The bout order is:  

3–6 2–5 1–5 1–4 2–4 2–6 1–6 3–5 3–4  

If this order is changed intentionally or accidentally, all hits scored after the change are annulled and the match continues in the correct order.  

**ORG-2.3.8**  
Team placement on the match score sheet is determined by drawing lots. The order of individual fencers is then decided by the team captains.  

#### Substitutions  

**ORG-2.3.9**  
During the match, the team captain may request to substitute a fencer with the reserve nominated before the start of the team match. This substitution may only be made at the end of a relay bout. The substituted fencer may fence once more during the match, but only to replace the fencer who originally replaced them. This second substitution is not permitted if the first substitution occurred for the reasons listed in the relevant article. No further substitution of a fencer currently on piste is permitted thereafter, even in case of accident or unavoidable circumstances. The request must be made at the latest before the start of the bout preceding the bout to be fenced by the fencer to be substituted, and the Referee must inform the opposing team captain.  

**ORG-2.3.10**  
If an accident occurs in the bout following a substitution request, the team captain may cancel the request.  

**ORG-2.3.11**  
If, consequently, the opposing team captain has also requested a substitution, the earlier request may be executed or cancelled.  

#### Withdrawal, exclusion  

**ORG-2.3.12**  
If a team member must withdraw during a match due to an accident properly recognised by the Organising Team’s medical representative, the team captain may request the reserve to enter the match at the point where the withdrawing fencer withdrew, even during an ongoing relay bout.  

**ORG-2.3.13**  
The same applies if a team member must be removed from the match due to equipment failure. See [GEN-6.2.13].  

**ORG-2.3.14**  
A fencer substituted in this manner may not take their place again in the team in the same match.  

**ORG-2.3.15**  
If both the fencer and the reserve (if any) must withdraw and the team is therefore incomplete, the team loses the match and must withdraw from the event, while retaining its placement in the final ranking.  

**ORG-2.3.16**  
If a fencer is excluded during a team match, their team loses the match and is excluded from the event without final placement. See [DIS-4.1.3].  

**ORG-2.3.17**  
If a team does not finish the event for any reason, the Organising Team applies the same rules as for an individual fencer failing to complete an event, treating the team as if it were a single competitor. See [DIS-4.3.20].  

**ORG-2.3.18**  
If a team does not appear to fence against another team:  
- If it has already fenced another team, it is treated as having not finished the event (see [ORG-2.2.4]);  
- If it does not appear for its first match, it is treated as not participating in the event at all.  

**ORG-2.3.19**  
For time and scoring limits in team matches, see the time/score framework rules under general rules.  

### Ranking / classification  
<span id="ORG-2.4"></span>  

**ORG-2.4.1**  
The overall ranking is established as follows:  
- 1st: winner of the 1st-place bout  
- 2nd: loser of the 1st-place bout  
- 3rd and 4th: the two semifinal losers fence for places (except where the final is run as direct elimination, in which case both are 3rd place)  
- All other placements correspond to the round reached in direct elimination.  

## Competition staff  
<span id="ORG-3"></span>  
<!-- TODO -->  

**ORG-3.1**  
Medical/first-aid staff provide examination and treatment of injuries or illness during and after bouts and provide professional opinion on immediate outcomes.  

**ORG-3.2**  
The head of the Organising Team is responsible for the smooth running of the competition. Complaints not relating to referee decisions (preferably in writing and on-site), as well as praise and suggestions before, during, and after the competition, should be directed to the Organising Team.  

**ORG-3.3**  
The Head Referee supervises the placement and performance of referees and assistant referees. In case of appeal where the Referee maintains their decision, the Head Referee may issue a decision. The Head Referee may personally, or via a delegated assistant, observe or review a referee’s work for educational or supervisory purposes.  

**ORG-3.4**  
The equipment-check team inspects protective equipment and weapons before the competition and records the fencers whose equipment was found compliant. Its leader is the Armourer.  

**ORG-3.5**  
The technical team operates the competition’s technical infrastructure, including event management software, and ensures proper documentation, storage, and display of event data.
