## Longsword / COMBAT rules  
<span id="LS-COMBAT-1"></span>  

### Judging hits  
<span id="LS-COMBAT-1.1"></span>  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.1**  
If either fencer receives a hit, the Referee immediately stops the bout.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.2**  
If, before the bout is stopped (i.e. before the call of "Halt!"), only one of the fencers is hit, **the fencer who landed the hit scores one point**.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.3**  
If, before the bout is stopped (i.e. before the call of "Halt!"), both fencers are hit and the Referee judges that the two hits did not occur at the same time (double hit), the fencer hit first in time is considered the one who was struck (**the hit that arrived first in time is considered a clean hit**). **The fencer who landed the hit scores one point**.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.4**  
If, before the bout is stopped (i.e. before the call of "Halt!"), both fencers are hit and the Referee judges that no time elapsed between the two hits (the two hits actually occurred simultaneously in time), there is a **simultaneous hit**, so both fencers are considered hit. **Both fencers receive 1 point**.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.5**  
Annulment of hits: if the Referee cannot judge whether a presumed hit actually occurred or whether it was valid, the Referee annuls the hit or the simultaneous hit and awards no point.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.1.6**  
- If there is a double hit in which one is valid and the other invalid (a hit that landed on an invalid surface, or that was delivered after leaving the piste, cf. [GEN-6.11.5]–[GEN-6.11.5] etc.), **only the valid hit shall be judged**.

### Optional rules  
<span id="LS-COMBAT-1.2"></span>  

**LS-COMBAT-1.2.1**  
The organiser decides on the application of the following rules. The activation of optional rules at a given competition must be announced independently for each rule, one by one, and published in the competition regulations.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.2.1.1**  
If, during a **direct-elimination bout, 5 simultaneous hits occur CONSECUTIVELY**:  

- the Referee selects, by drawing lots (coin toss), the fencer who is favoured in the next exchange;  
- if the result of the next exchange is a simultaneous hit, only the favoured fencer scores one point and the opponent's hit must be annulled;  
- the favoured fencer changes from exchange to exchange, regardless of the outcome of the previous exchange, until the end of the bout;  
- if both fencers reach 10 hits simultaneously through joint hits, or if the score is tied when time expires, this favoured-fencer status must be cancelled and the general rules applied (cf. [GEN-6.12.5.1.2], [GEN-6.12.5.4]);  
- this rule does not apply in pool bouts.  

**LS-COMBAT-1.2.1.2**  
If a fencer **strikes/thrusts at the opponent who landed a valid hit on them**, and the opponent parries this and again validly cuts or thrusts them, two hits are counted against them — **the opponent scores two points**.  

- Applicable to immediate action / after-action:  
    - the attack lands, the defender ripostes immediately after a failed (early/late/missed) parry or after defending by distance unsuccessfully → the fencer who landed the hit parries this and delivers a counter-riposte;  
    - the counter-attack lands, but the attacker still completes the simple or compound attack already initiated → the fencer who landed with the counter-attack parries this and ripostes.  
- The Referee must not call "Halt!" too early, so that any potential riposte may remain valid. The purpose of the rule is to reward the parrying of actions that are still relevant to the original fencing phrase and hit.  
- Applicable only to a single (after-)cut/thrust; any further actions must be disregarded.  
- If this 'after-riposte' does not land or is not valid, the second point is not awarded.  
- Initiating an action after the call of "Halt!" falls under the first group of disciplinary offences in accordance with the general rules (03-General).  
- _(See: Lovag Arlow Gusztáv – A Kardvívás, Chapter XVI, §48 The Rules of the Bout, 18.)_
